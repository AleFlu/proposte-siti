Colour blindness simulator

Load a photo and see it the way a person with a protan, deutan or tritan deficiency sees it, at the severity you choose.

How I see itOriginal

The filter uses the physiological model by Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009), applied in linear RGB. A person with normal vision looking at the filtered part sees roughly what you see.

Save and share

Save the filtered image

Prepare the image, then press and hold it (phone) or right-click → “Save image”. You can also copy it to the clipboard.

Share your profile

Anyone who opens this link sees the simulator already set to your profile and can upload their own photos.
